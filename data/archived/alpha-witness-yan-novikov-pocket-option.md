## Statistical audit

The creator’s own minimum of 1,000–2,000 trades makes the session visibly inadequate:

- ten planned trades represent **1.0% of 1,000 trades**;
- ten planned trades represent **0.5% of 2,000 trades**.

Even if ten independent trades produced six wins, the uncertainty would remain too wide to distinguish a durable edge from chance. The independence assumption is itself doubtful because the trades occur in one short session, potentially under similar market conditions.

For a binary option that loses the full stake on failure and earns a net payout fraction `p` on success, the break-even win rate is:

> **Break-even win rate = 1 / (1 + p)**

| Net winning payout | Minimum break-even win rate |
|---:|---:|
| 70% | 58.8% |
| 80% | 55.6% |
| 90% | 52.6% |
| 100% | 50.0% |

Therefore, the hypothesized 60% win rate would be positive only if the net payout exceeded approximately **66.7%**, before execution errors and other frictions. The actual contract-by-contract payout is **NON AVAILABLE**, so realized expectancy is **NON AVAILABLE**.
